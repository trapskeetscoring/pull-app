# Pull! Support

_Updated on October 10, 2026_

Thanks for using Pull!.

## Contact

**trapskeetscoring@gmail.com**

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use Pull!?

No. There is no account or sign-up. When a league is shared with you, you choose which member you are and the league's secretary confirms it. Until they do, your name shows as *unconfirmed*; once a member is confirmed, no other device can take that name.

### What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. iCloud is needed only for sharing and syncing.

### Does Pull! work without iCloud?

Yes. Pull! works fully offline. iCloud is only required if you want to sync a league across multiple devices or share a league with other people.

### Does Pull! work with VoiceOver?

No. Pull! is not designed for VoiceOver, and VoiceOver is not supported. It does follow the larger
text sizes you can set in iOS — see **Text size and VoiceOver** in the user guide.

### How do I share a league with my team?

From the league screen, tap the **"…"** button in the top right and choose **Share**. Both you and each recipient must be signed into iCloud. You can set each collaborator to read-only or read-write, and only the person who created the league can invite or stop sharing.

### Can I export my data?

Yes, in two places. **Reports** produces PDFs of standings, results, rosters, leaders, the schedule, and an all-members grid. For the raw season data, use the **"…"** menu on the league screen → **Export League**, which offers CSV and JSON.

JSON is the complete one — every team, member, score, week and setting, and the file the app can import back. CSV covers scores and rosters and is the one to open in a spreadsheet.

### How are scores corrected after submission?

The league secretary can edit any approved score directly, from **Scoring Data → View / Edit** on the league screen. Captains submit score sheets that the secretary reviews and approves, and an approved sheet keeps the scorekeeper's original entries.

If a sheet was approved too early, the secretary opens that week under **Scoring Data → View / Edit**, pages to the team, taps **Shot-by-Shot Detail**, and chooses **Return to Scorekeeper**. That removes the week's scores for that team and gives the sheet back to the person who kept score, with their grid as they left it, so the fix is an edit rather than a re-entry.

### My league isn't showing on my other device — what's wrong?

Confirm both devices are signed into the same iCloud account, and that Pull! is switched on in the list of apps using iCloud (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud).

A cloud icon appears at the top left of the Leagues list only when something needs attention — no icon means sync is healthy. For details and a manual sync, tap the **gear** on the Leagues list and choose **iCloud Sync**; if anything has failed, a **Last Sync Error** row there gives the actual reason, and **Sync Now** on the same screen forces a fetch. It lives on the Leagues list rather than inside a league so that it is still reachable when you have just accepted a share invitation and have no league to open yet.

If Sync Now reports nothing and the league still has not arrived, use **Re-read Everything from iCloud** on that same screen. iCloud hands out each change once, so a change this device asked for but could not use is spent — after that Sync Now truthfully says there is nothing new, which looks the same as nothing being wrong. Re-read starts over and reads everything again. It costs a download and nothing else.

Note that a sync problem can mean *your* latest change has not reached anyone else, not just that theirs has not reached you — worth checking before a match night rather than after.

### Can I use Pull! on my iPhone and my iPad at the same time?

Yes — any number of devices can hold the same league, and changes to different things on different devices are merged. The one thing to avoid is **two secretary devices changing scores or members at the same time**: every member's details and scores travel together, so the device that saves later keeps its version and the other's changes are lost. Enter and approve a night's scores on **one** device, and let it finish before scores are touched anywhere else. Captains submitting score sheets never collide with each other or with you. The user guide's *Using more than one secretary device* (Section 14) has the details.

## Reporting a Bug

Please include:

- iPhone or iPad model and iOS version
- A description of what you were doing when the problem occurred
- The exact text of any error message
- Screenshots if applicable
- For a sync problem, the event log: gear on the Leagues list → **iCloud Sync** → **Export Event Log**. It holds times, statuses and internal ids only — no names, contact details or scores.
